Method note: the counter reset (echo 0 > /proc/fs/cifs/Stats) needs root, which
was not available here, so this uses a before/after delta of the world-readable
counters instead of a reset. /proc/fs/cifs/Stats was snapshotted, one scan run,
then snapshotted again; the deltas below are exact. The mount was kept quiet, but
a delta cannot exclude all background activity the way a reset-and-read can, so
treat small deltas (single-thousands) as having some noise floor.

Run (one discarded warmup walk + one measured walk = two walks):
  target/release/examples/scan_bench \
    --root /mnt/jane-nas/Entertainment/Audiobooks \
    --mode gaps --iterations 1 --concurrency 1 --no-save --label roundtrip
  -> dirs_visited=901  entries_seen=8802   (x2 walks: 1802 dir-visits, 17604 entry-visits)
